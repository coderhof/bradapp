For best results, photograph one flat page at a time, keep the camera parallel to the page, use bright even light, and crop away the table/background. How capture works

The image is processed in your browser using a free OCR library loaded from a public CDN; the photo is not uploaded to a BradApp server by this app. The first use needs an internet connection to load the library. OCR can misread numbers and fractions, so always verify the recipe before saving.
